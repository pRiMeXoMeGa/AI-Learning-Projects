## The budget versus goal tension

A VC-scale target with a $1k–10k runway means the plan has three phases:

1. **Validate cheaply.** MVP in 4–8 weeks with low-cost models and organic distribution.
2. **Show a fundable signal.** Paying users, retention above the AI-app benchmark, organic growth.
3. **Raise pre-seed or seed** (an accelerator such as YC, or angels) to fund scaling.

This rules out ideas where each free user costs dollars, such as unlimited
free AI video generation. It also rules out ideas that need large up-front
spend before the first signal, such as hardware or regulated clinical products.
