## D. Cautionary tales from 2026

| What happened | Why it matters |
|---------------|----------------|
| **OpenAI shut down the Sora app** (announced March 2026, six months after launch). Reportedly about $1M a day in compute, falling downloads (-32% month over month in December), and legal issues | Pure entertainment AI video with free generation does not pay for its compute, even for OpenAI. Don't build "AI slop feeds". Build tools where users pay per output. |
| **Google's Nano Banana** (Gemini image editing) took over casual photo editing | Generic image editing and headshot apps are being commoditized by the labs. |
| **California SB 243** (in force 1 Jan 2026) regulates companion chatbots for minors. **Illinois, Nevada and Utah** restrict AI therapy | Companion and therapy apps carry growing legal and reputational risk. |
| **568+ new "GLP-1" apps** launched since January 2026 | A hot trend can fill with copycats within months. Be the coach, not another tracker. |

## Patterns across the winners

1. **Prosumer beats pure consumer.** Users who make money or save time from the output (Higgsfield, Lovable, ElevenLabs, Gamma) pay $20–100+ a month.
2. **Workflow and format layers over commoditizing models.** Higgsfield doesn't depend on any single model. It wins on presets, speed and taste.
3. **Voice is the breakout modality of 2026.** ElevenLabs, Speak, Wispr and Plaud are all voice-first and all growing fast.
4. **Tiny teams can win** (Cal AI with 7 people, Gamma with ~50) when one AI interaction feels magical and distribution is creator- or TikTok-led.
5. **Products that make an expensive human service cheap** (tutor, coach, matchmaker, trainer) are a recurring theme in investor theses.
