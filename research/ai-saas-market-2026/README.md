## Summary

**Most attractive niche: voice-first AI coaching for skills with an economic payoff.**
This means AI that replaces an expensive human coach or tutor, using real-time voice.

Why this niche, in one paragraph: voice is the modality with the strongest
growth evidence in 2026 (ElevenLabs at a reported $600M ARR, Speak at a reported
$100M ARR, Wispr at a $2B valuation). Real-time voice API costs have fallen to
roughly $0.02–0.05 per minute, so a $1k–10k budget can fund a working product.
People keep paying when they can see measurable progress, which counters the
biggest weakness of consumer AI apps: they churn about 36% faster than non-AI apps.
Foundation labs offer generic voice chat, but they don't build curricula, scoring
or progress tracking for a specific skill. That gap is where a startup can win.

### The 3 ideas

| # | Idea | Modality | Weighted niche score | Verdict |
|---|------|----------|----------------------|---------|
| 1 | **Voice coach for non-native English professionals** (job interviews, meetings, presentations) | Audio + text | 3.95 / 5 | **Recommended** |
| 2 | **AI promo studio for independent musicians** (track → lyric video, Spotify Canvas, Shorts/Reels clips, cover art) | Video + image + audio | 3.50 / 5 | Highest growth upside, highest cost and willingness-to-pay risk |
| 3 | **Camera + voice AI strength coach** (phone watches your form and talks to you; first market is GLP-1 users who need to keep muscle) | Vision + voice | 3.60 / 5 | Lowest inference cost; crowded fitness market |

### One tension to resolve

You chose a **VC-scale** goal with a **$1k–10k** budget. That works only if the
budget is spent on reaching fundable milestones fast: a real product, paying
users and a retention curve. It should not be spent on growth itself. Each idea
in [05](05-saas-ideas.md) includes the traction milestones that would make a
pre-seed or seed raise realistic. Consumer startups make up only about 2–5% of
recent Y Combinator batches, and YC is explicitly asking for consumer AI, so a
strong consumer team faces less competition for investor attention.

## Method and caveats

- Sources: 2026 press coverage, Sensor Tower, RevenueCat and Appfigures data
  (as reported in coverage), investor theses from a16z and Y Combinator, and
  published API pricing. Full list in [sources.md](sources.md).
- Revenue and valuation figures are **as reported** by the press or company
  statements. Private-company figures are not audited and some are estimates.
- Some primary sites (a16z.com, sensortower.com, revenuecat.com, techcrunch.com)
  could not be opened directly from the research environment. Their figures
  come from search summaries and secondary coverage, so check them before
  quoting them to investors.
- The niche scores are a judgement, made against weights you can change
  (see [04](04-niche-analysis.md)).
